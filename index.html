Our Culinary Story

A Taste of Elegance

Armania - Cafe & Pub is a culinary haven that caters to the refined tastes of Sansdhani of Jabalpur. Perched atop Hotel Rishi Regency, we provide a vibrant yet sophisticated escape with an expansive panoramic city view.

We take pride in being one of the city's select FL2-licensed rooftop venues, serving an eclectic array spanning authentic Indian curries, Asian delicacies, Italian classics, and smoky live bar-be-cue creations.
